Synopsis

In the subterranean megalopolis of Sector Zero, memory architect Elena Rostova stumbles upon a dormant synthetic consciousness known as Protocol 99. Pursued by high-table cybernetic cartels and the rogue peacekeepers of the Red Line, she forms a volatile alliance with augmented ex-sentinel Kaelen to expose a syndicate weapon capable of deleting digital human identity at will.
